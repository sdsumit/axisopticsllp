What Vantap does

A small set of things, done properly, rather than a bloated dashboard.

01

API your CRM can call

A straightforward API for sending messages, checking delivery status and managing templates, so your developer or CRM vendor can integrate it without rebuilding your workflow.

02

Consent built in, not bolted on

Every contact's opt-in status is tracked and stored. Opt-outs are honored automatically, so your business stays inside Meta's messaging policy without manual bookkeeping.

03

Template management

Create and submit message templates for marketing, order updates and account notifications, and track their approval status in one screen.

04

Delivery visibility

See what was sent, delivered, read or failed, per contact and per campaign — logged on our side, not left to disappear after a few weeks.

05

Your own WhatsApp number

Messages send from your business's own verified WhatsApp Business Account. You keep the relationship with your customers; Vantap is the pipe, not the middleman.

06

Clear costs

Meta's per-message charges are billed directly to your business by Meta. Vantap's fee is separate and shown up front, so there's no hidden markup on message costs.

Built for SMBs already running a CRM
