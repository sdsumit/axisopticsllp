Privacy Policy

Applies to the Vantap website and messaging platform, operated by Axis Optics LLP. Last updated: September 2026.

This policy covers Vantap only. It does not apply to the Axis Optics lens distribution website or business.

Who we are

Vantap is operated by Axis Optics LLP, registered at 234 Pilibhit Bypass Road, Ashish Royal Park, Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh 243006, India. Contact: contact@axisopticsllp.com.

What we collect

  • From businesses using Vantap: business name, contact details, WhatsApp Business Account information, and billing details needed to operate the service.
  • From a business's own contacts (their customers): phone numbers, opt-in and opt-out status, message content and template data, and delivery status (sent, delivered, read, failed), so that messages can be sent and tracked on a business's behalf.
  • From our website: standard analytics data such as pages visited and general location, used to understand site usage.

How we use it

  • To send and deliver messages through the WhatsApp Business Platform on behalf of the business using Vantap.
  • To track delivery status, opt-in and opt-out records, and template approval status.
  • To bill for the Vantap service and provide support.
  • To meet our obligations under Meta's WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy and applicable law.

Who this data is shared with

Message data is shared with Meta (WhatsApp) as required to deliver messages through the WhatsApp Business Platform. We do not sell contact or message data to third parties.

Retention

We retain delivery logs and opt-in records for as long as a business's account with Vantap is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to meet legal and accounting requirements. A business using Vantap can request deletion of its data by contacting us.

Consent and opt-outs

Businesses using Vantap are responsible for obtaining valid opt-in consent from their own contacts before sending marketing messages, in line with Meta's WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy. Opt-out requests are honored and removed from active messaging lists.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting contact@axisopticsllp.com. If you are a customer of a business using Vantap and want your number removed, you can also opt out directly by replying STOP, or by contacting that business.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service develops. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
